## 2026-07-08 - Accessible Streamlit Inputs and Buttons via Tooltips
**Learning:** In Streamlit, inputs and buttons lack native ARIA label parameters. However, using the `help` parameter adds a native tooltip that acts as an implicit accessibility description, providing crucial context for screen readers and improving overall UX for ambiguous elements (like icon-only buttons or complex inputs).
**Action:** Always include a clear, descriptive `help` parameter when creating `st.text_input`, `st.button`, `st.multiselect`, `st.file_uploader`, and `st.form_submit_button` widgets to ensure they meet basic accessibility and interaction standards.
